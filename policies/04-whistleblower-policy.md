> **Plain-language summary.** If you see something wrong, report it. No one will be punished for an honest report. When the concern involves a founder or officer, it goes to someone independent of them.

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy encourages good-faith reports of suspected wrongdoing and protects people who make them. It supports TENNISSA's Form 990 governance disclosures and is consistent with federal and Tennessee anti-retaliation laws, including:

- 18 U.S.C. § 1513(e), which makes it a crime to retaliate against someone for giving truthful information about a possible federal offense to law enforcement; and
- the Tennessee Public Protection Act, Tenn. Code Ann. § 50-1-304, which protects employees who refuse to take part in, or remain silent about, illegal activities.

## 2. Who May Report

Anyone may report under this policy: directors, officers, employees, volunteers, contractors, families, schools, donors and members of the public.

## 3. What to Report

Report suspected:

- fraud, theft or misuse of scholarship or operating funds;
- improper scholarship selection, manipulation of eligibility or income documentation, or awards to disqualified persons;
- donor earmarking, tuition-swap arrangements or quid-pro-quo gifts;
- undisclosed conflicts or related-party transactions;
- false financial records, donor statements or regulatory filings (IRS, TDOE, Tennessee Secretary of State);
- violations of Section 25F, other laws, donor restrictions or TENNISSA policies;
- misuse or unauthorized disclosure of student or donor data;
- a participating school misrepresenting enrollment, tuition or accreditation; and
- retaliation against anyone who raised a concern.

## 4. How to Report

| If the concern involves… | Report to |
|---|---|
| Anyone other than those below | The Board Chair or the designated Compliance Contact |
| The Board Chair or the Executive Director | Any director who is not involved, or independent counsel designated by the Board |
| A Founding Director, or a majority of the Board | Independent counsel or an outside professional designated by the Board for this purpose (named on the website) |

Reports may be written, oral or **anonymous**. Anonymous reports are made through `[confidential reporting address or form, to be designated]`. Anonymous reporting can make follow-up harder, so include enough detail to investigate.

**External reporting.** Nothing in this policy limits anyone's right to report directly to a government agency, including:

- the IRS (Form 13909, Tax-Exempt Organization Complaint/Referral);
- the Tennessee Attorney General;
- the Tennessee Secretary of State, Division of Charitable Solicitations;
- the Tennessee Department of Education; or
- law enforcement.

No agreement with TENNISSA may prohibit such a report.

> *Counsel note.* Version 4.0 sent reports to the Board Chair or a "disinterested director." TENNISSA's three Founding Directors hold permanent seats and include the Executive Director. Concerns about founders therefore need a route that does not depend on the other founders. We recommend naming a standing independent contact, such as outside counsel or a CPA, and, as soon as practical, filling the two open board seats with independent directors.

## 5. Good Faith

A report must be based on a reasonable belief that wrongdoing may have occurred. You do not need proof. A person who knowingly makes a false report may face corrective action.

## 6. No Retaliation

TENNISSA prohibits retaliation against anyone who, in good faith:

- makes a report;
- takes part in an investigation;
- gives information to a regulator or law enforcement; or
- refuses to take part in an activity they reasonably believe is illegal.

Retaliation includes threats, harassment, dismissal, demotion, loss of volunteer roles, and adverse action on a scholarship application or school participation. Anyone who retaliates is subject to corrective action, up to removal from their position as permitted by the Bylaws and law.

## 7. Investigation

1. **Acknowledge** the report within **5 business days**, if the reporter can be contacted.
2. **Assess** whether it falls under this policy, and issue a legal hold under [REC-001 §5](03-records-retention-policy.md) if needed.
3. **Investigate** promptly and fairly. Counsel, accountants or other independent professionals may be engaged. A person implicated in the report may not control, direct or be informed of the investigation's details beyond what fairness requires.
4. **Conclude**, normally within **60 days**, with written findings. Where appropriate, the reporter is told that the matter has been addressed.
5. **Report** to the Board, or to the disinterested directors, in summary at the next meeting. Serious matters are reported immediately.

## 8. Confidentiality

The reporter's identity and the report are shared only with people who need them to investigate or resolve the matter, or as the law requires. Complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

## 9. Corrective Action

Corrective action may include:

- recovery of funds;
- correcting donor statements or government filings;
- added controls;
- discipline or removal (Founding Directors only as permitted by Bylaws Section 4.4 and the law);
- suspending a school;
- revising policies; and
- notifying authorities where required.

## 10. Records and Review

Reports and investigation files are kept under the [Records Retention Policy](03-records-retention-policy.md). This policy is distributed every year to directors, officers, staff and volunteers. The Board reviews it every year.
