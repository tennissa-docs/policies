## 1. Purpose

This policy protects personal and confidential information about applicants, students, families, donors, schools, directors, staff and volunteers.

## 2. Protected Information

Protected information includes:

- student and family identity, contact, enrollment and household information;
- household income documents, tax transcripts, benefit-program records and scholarship applications;
- student education records obtained from schools, including disability-related information;
- EFS/ESA/IEA participation and award information;
- donor identity, contribution amounts, Section 25F donor identifiers and tax documents;
- bank account, card and payment information;
- compensation records and conflict disclosures; and
- passwords, credentials and confidential organizational records.

## 3. Collection and Use

1. **Minimum necessary.** TENNISSA collects only what it needs to administer scholarships, meet Section 25F and other legal obligations, process gifts, and operate the organization.
2. **No unnecessary Social Security numbers.** TENNISSA does not collect full Social Security numbers unless the law requires them. It uses redacted documents, IRS transcripts obtained with consent, or IRS-issued donor identifiers instead.
3. **Purpose limitation.** Information is used only for the purpose for which it was collected. EFS/ESA/IEA information is used only to coordinate awards under SCH-001 §4.4.
4. **Notice.** Application, donation and website forms link to a public privacy notice. The notice explains what is collected, why, who receives it (schools, platform vendors, auditors, the IRS and TDOE as required) and how long it is kept.
5. **Children.** TENNISSA's website and online forms are directed to parents and adults, not to children under 13. Applications are submitted by a parent or guardian.

## 4. Student Education Records (FERPA)

Participating schools that receive federal funds may be subject to the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). TENNISSA does not ask a school to disclose a student's education records without a **signed written consent from the parent** (or from an eligible student aged 18 or older). That consent is collected as part of the application and describes the records to be shared and why. School participation agreements require schools to share only the minimum necessary, such as enrollment confirmation and tuition charges.

## 5. Access and Security

1. Access is role-based and limited to people with a need to know. Scholarship reviewers see only the applications assigned to them.
2. Required safeguards:
   - multi-factor authentication on email, the scholarship and donation platform, banking and cloud storage;
   - unique accounts with no shared logins;
   - encryption of devices and of files in transit;
   - automatic screen locking; and
   - removal of access within **2 business days** after a person's role ends.
3. Protected information may not be stored in personal email or personal cloud accounts, or sent by unencrypted email attachment, when a secure alternative is available.
4. The website's interest form collects only contact information and interest type. It must not be used to collect income documents or student records.

## 6. Vendors and Service Providers

Vendors that store or process protected information (for example FACTS Compass, payment processors, form services, accountants and auditors) must:

- be approved by the Executive Director after reviewing their security practices;
- sign a written agreement requiring confidentiality, appropriate security, use only for TENNISSA's purposes, prompt breach notice to TENNISSA, and return or deletion of data at the end of the contract; and
- keep their data within the United States unless the Board approves otherwise.

## 7. Sharing and Disclosure

Information may be shared only:

- with participating schools, to confirm enrollment and apply awards;
- with vendors under §6;
- with auditors, counsel and the IRS, TDOE or other agencies as the law requires; or
- with the consent of the person concerned.

TENNISSA **never sells, rents or trades** personal information. Names, photographs or stories of students or donors are used publicly only with written consent, given by a parent for minors.

## 8. Donor Information

Donor identities and amounts are confidential. Donor names are excluded from public copies of Form 990 (Schedule B). A donor listed publicly, for example on a donor wall, must have opted in. Donors are never told which students their gifts funded.

## 9. Retention and Disposal

Information is kept and destroyed according to the [Records Retention Policy](03-records-retention-policy.md).

## 10. Security Incidents and Breach Notification

1. Anyone who suspects loss, theft, unauthorized access, disclosure or alteration of protected information must report it **immediately** to the Privacy Officer, or to a disinterested director if the Privacy Officer is involved.
2. The Privacy Officer, with counsel, will:
   - **contain** the incident, for example by resetting credentials, revoking access or notifying vendors;
   - **assess** what data and which people are affected;
   - **notify** affected individuals as the law requires. Tennessee's breach-notification statute, Tenn. Code Ann. § 47-18-2107, generally requires notice to affected Tennessee residents **within 45 days** after a breach of unencrypted personal information is discovered, and notice to consumer reporting agencies when more than 1,000 people are notified. Other states' laws apply to residents of those states. TENNISSA will also notify schools, the IRS or TDOE where required or appropriate;
   - **document** the incident, the response and the lessons learned; and
   - **report** to the Board.
3. Vendors must notify TENNISSA of any incident affecting TENNISSA data promptly, and no later than the time stated in their contract.

## 11. Confidentiality and Training

1. Directors, officers, staff, volunteers, reviewers and contractors sign a confidentiality agreement and must protect information during and after their service.
2. Everyone with access to protected information completes privacy and security training when they start and every year after that.
3. Violations may lead to loss of access, removal from a role, or other corrective action.

## 12. Individual Requests

Parents, applicants and donors may ask to see or correct the information TENNISSA holds about them, or ask how it is used. TENNISSA responds within 30 days, subject to legal retention duties.

## 13. Review

The Board reviews this policy every year, after any significant incident, and whenever applicable law changes.
