> **Why this policy was added.** Version 4.0 mentioned Section 25F only in passing. Keeping TENNISSA's place on Tennessee's list, and protecting donors' credits, depends on a specific set of federal requirements. Each requirement needs an owner and a deadline. This policy gathers them in one place.

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy assigns responsibility for TENNISSA's ongoing compliance with IRC § 25F, its Treasury regulations and IRS guidance, and Tennessee's requirements for scholarship granting organizations (SGOs).

> **Important.** Section 25F is new. Its regulations and IRS procedures (forms, portal, deadlines) may change. Every number and date in this policy must be **checked each year** against current IRS and TDOE guidance before it is relied on. If this policy conflicts with current law, the law controls, and the Compliance Director proposes an update.

## 2. Status Requirements

TENNISSA must maintain all of the following at all times:

| Requirement | Evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Tax-exempt status under IRC § 501(c)(3) (and not a private foundation) | IRS determination letter; timely Form 990 filings | Executive Director |
| Listed by Tennessee as a qualified SGO | TDOE certification or approval and inclusion on the state's list provided to Treasury | Compliance Director |
| Registered with the IRS as an SGO | IRS SGO portal registration confirmation | Compliance Director |
| Tennessee charitable solicitation registration or exemption | Secretary of State filing | Executive Director |
| Good standing with the Tennessee Secretary of State | Annual report filed | Executive Director |

Credit-eligible contributions are accepted **only** while every requirement above is current (see [DON-001 §3](05-donor-acceptance-policy.md)). If any requirement lapses, the Compliance Director tells the Board immediately, and TENNISSA stops describing contributions as credit-eligible until the requirement is restored.

## 3. Core Operating Requirements

| # | Requirement | How TENNISSA complies |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Separate account for Section 25F funds | [FIN-001 §6](02-financial-controls-policy.md) |
| 2 | At least 90% of income spent on scholarships for eligible students within the required period | [FIN-001 §6.3](02-financial-controls-policy.md) receipt-year schedule |
| 3 | Scholarships only for eligible students (income ≤ 300% of area median gross income, eligible to attend public school, Tennessee resident) | [SCH-001 §4](01-scholarship-policy.md) |
| 4 | Scholarships available at more than one school, to at least the required minimum number of students | [SCH-001 §5.3](01-scholarship-policy.md) |
| 5 | Priority for renewing students and their siblings | [SCH-001 §5.1](01-scholarship-policy.md) |
| 6 | No scholarships to disqualified persons | [SCH-001 §4.6](01-scholarship-policy.md), [COI-001 §4](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md) |
| 7 | No earmarking; no donor selection of students | [DON-001 §§4–5](05-donor-acceptance-policy.md) |
| 8 | Only qualified education expenses; payments to schools | [SCH-001 §7](01-scholarship-policy.md) |
| 9 | Only individual cash contributions treated as credit-eligible | [DON-001 §3](05-donor-acceptance-policy.md) |
| 10 | Donor statements, IRS reports and the annual audit | §4 below |

## 4. Annual Compliance Calendar

*Dates reflect IRS guidance as of October 2026. Confirm them every year.*

| When | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Before first solicitation** | Confirm TDOE listing and IRS SGO registration. Confirm Tennessee charitable solicitation registration or exemption. Approve the public credit disclosures. | Compliance Director |
| **Ongoing** | Record each Section 25F contribution with the information needed for donor statements. Deposit it only into the Section 25F account. | Financial Controller Director |
| **December 31** | Last day for contributions counted in the current calendar year. Website and materials must state this deadline clearly. | Executive Director |
| **By January 31** | Issue the Section 25F written statement to each donor for the prior year, in the IRS-prescribed form and including the IRS donor identifier, so donors can file **Form 8525** (or its successor). | Compliance Director |
| **By February 28** | File the annual contribution report through the IRS SGO portal, or as the IRS directs. | Compliance Director |
| **Quarterly** | Update the 90% spending schedule and give it to the Board. Reconcile the Section 25F account. | Financial Controller Director |
| **Each award cycle** | Reverify schools. Verify student eligibility. Screen for disqualified persons. Apply renewal and sibling priority. | Executive Director |
| **After fiscal year-end** | Complete the **annual independent audit** required under Section 25F. Under current guidance this covers the SGO's finances and its compliance with the program rules. Smaller SGOs may use a reduced form of review if the IRS allows it. The Board's disinterested directors receive the report. | Financial Controller Director |
| **Form 990 due date (or as IRS directs, currently May 15 for calendar-year filers)** | File Form 990 with any required Section 25F schedule or compliance information. | Financial Controller Director |
| **By the Tennessee deadline (generally 6 months after year-end)** | Renew Tennessee charitable solicitation registration or exemption. | Executive Director |
| **January** | Collect annual conflict-of-interest and disqualified-person statements ([COI-001 Appendix A](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md)). Update the substantial-contributor list. | Board Chair |
| **Annually** | Board reviews all eight policies and this calendar. | Board |

## 5. Corrections and Errors

1. If TENNISSA discovers an error in a donor statement, an IRS report or a scholarship award, the Compliance Director tells the Executive Director and the Board Chair within **5 business days**.
2. With CPA or counsel guidance, TENNISSA issues corrected donor statements and amended reports as the IRS procedures require. It does so promptly enough that donors can correct their returns.
3. If a scholarship was paid to an ineligible student, the [Scholarship Policy](01-scholarship-policy.md) §10 applies. The Board decides with counsel whether any corrective payment or disclosure is required.

## 6. Public Communications

Everything that describes the credit (website, flyers, postcards, emails, videos) must:

- state that the credit is **nonrefundable**, subject to the **per-taxpayer cap** (currently $1,700) and to each donor's own tax liability, with any unused amount carried forward as the law allows;
- state that contributions must be **cash from individuals**;
- state the **status** of TDOE listing and IRS registration until both are complete;
- avoid "free," "zero cost," or "this is not a donation" unless the qualifier in [DON-001 §10](05-donor-acceptance-policy.md) appears next to it; and
- include "TENNISSA does not provide tax advice; consult your tax professional."

The Compliance Director approves new credit-related materials before they are published.

## 7. Platform Use

TENNISSA uses a third-party SGO platform (currently FACTS Compass) for applications, gifts and statements. Using a vendor does not shift TENNISSA's legal responsibility. The Compliance Director:

- confirms each year that the platform's donor statements and reports match current IRS formats;
- reconciles platform reports to the bank and the general ledger before each filing; and
- confirms that a data-protection agreement is in place under [PRIV-001 §6](06-privacy-confidentiality-policy.md).

## 8. Reporting to the Board

At every regular meeting, the Compliance Director gives a short compliance dashboard covering:

- status of each requirement in §2;
- the 90% spending schedule;
- upcoming calendar items;
- any errors or corrections; and
- any regulatory changes.

## 9. Review

The Board reviews this policy every year, and within 60 days after any new Section 25F regulations, IRS procedures or TDOE rules.
