# Changelog

All notable changes to the TENNISSA Policy and Procedures Manual.

## [5.0] — Draft, October 2026 (pending counsel review and Board adoption)

### Added
- **SGO-001 Section 25F SGO Compliance and Reporting Policy**, a new policy. It covers status requirements, the annual compliance calendar, corrections, public-messaging rules, oversight of the FACTS Compass platform, and the Board compliance dashboard.
- Section 25F disqualified-person bar (SCH-001 §4.6, COI-001 §4).
- Student income test (300% of area median gross income), verification methods, and no SSN collection (SCH-001 §§4.1–4.2).
- Renewal, then sibling, then need-based priority, plus the more-than-one-school breadth rule (SCH-001 §5).
- Segregated Section 25F bank account and a 90% receipt-year spending schedule (FIN-001 §6).
- Annual independent audit, crime/fidelity insurance, and a ban on loans to insiders (FIN-001 §§5, 10).
- Credit-eligibility rules for gifts: only cash from individuals, and only after state listing and IRS registration (DON-001 §3).
- Ban on tuition swaps and quid-pro-quo gifts, and a designation table (DON-001 §§4–5).
- § 170(f)(8) and § 6115 acknowledgments, and Tennessee charitable-solicitation registration (DON-001 §§7, 10).
- FERPA consent, vendor agreements, the Tennessee 45-day breach-notice rule, and individual requests (PRIV-001).
- 10-year retention for Section 25F records, permanent retention for Form 1023 and Form 990s, and public-disclosure duties (REC-001).
- Independent whistleblower contact, external reporting channels and timelines (WB-001).
- Tennessee two-qualified-director rule, a board-composition section, periodic review, and Appendix A disclosure form (COI-001).
- Board adoption resolution with decisions D1–D9.

### Changed
- School eligibility changed from "TDOE Category III only" to **Category I, II or III**, plus active CISNA accreditation. An optional CISNA-candidacy transition is offered (Board decision D1).
- The EFS/ESA "qualifies-for" exclusion is replaced by a **coordination (last-dollar) rule** (SCH-001 §4.4).
- Scholarship payments must go directly to schools, no longer "where appropriate" (SCH-001 §7.2).
- Insider compensation must be fixed, reasonable and § 4958-documented, and is paid from operating funds only.

### Removed
- Executive Director compensation "based on annual scholarship funds administered" (formerly FIN-001 §11 and COI-001 §11).
