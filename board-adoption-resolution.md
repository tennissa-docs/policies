**WHEREAS**, TENNISSA is a Tennessee nonprofit corporation organized for charitable and educational purposes and intends to operate as a scholarship granting organization under Section 25F of the Internal Revenue Code and applicable Tennessee law;

**WHEREAS**, the Board has reviewed the Policy and Procedures Manual, Version 5.0, consisting of the policies listed below, together with the accompanying Legal Review Notes;
